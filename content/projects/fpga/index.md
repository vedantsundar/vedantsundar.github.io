The purpose of this investigation is to design a Now Serving Display circuit for a subsidiary of [redacted] to track customers served by delicatessen employees before signaling employees to take a break. The scope of this research encompasses Kitchen Display System design remediation nationwide.

A Design Brief derived from the Request For Proposal and Preliminary Schematics provided will be used to virtually design and simulate a Now Serving Display circuit, on the National Instruments Multisim Circuit Design Software, prior to actual breadboarding. The final circuit prototype will be implemented on a mounted Field Programmable Gate Array framework, and every possible input will be repeatedly tested to ensure design accuracy.

The Now Serving Display circuit implemented on the Protoboard functioned with perfect accuracy and precision throughout all trials, successfully counting from 0 to 85 to signal a break for delicatessen counter employees and offering reset capabilities. This design will increase delicatessen employee satisfaction and performance, leading to decreased Kitchen Display System malfunctions and increased profits for [redacted] upon commercial implementation. It is recommended that the client use the findings of this investigation to optimize Kitchen Display Systems with the designed circuit.

A full ANSI/NISO Z.39 (military-style) Technical Report can be accessed at the top of this page.

This Independent Research Project was completed at home and at an Engineering Lab. My advisor (of credentials USAF SQ/CC, but whom I will not name for privacy reasons) had provided the problem to me via a Request for Proposal (RFP); I was contracted (in an unofficial capacity) by his company to conduct a portion of his project that applied to Kitchen Display Systems. My advisor was generous enough in providing guidance and resources / materials needed for the investigation (*e*.*g*. a protoboard, Multisim simulation software access, etc).
